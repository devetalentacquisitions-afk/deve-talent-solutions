Junior Quantity Surveyor

Construction London Permanent Full-Time
£30,000+ per annum + Benefits

We are seeking a motivated and enthusiastic Junior Quantity Surveyor to join our client's growing commercial team within the roofing construction industry. This is an excellent opportunity for a recent graduate or someone with 1–3 years' experience looking to develop their career in quantity surveying.

Working alongside experienced Quantity Surveyors and Project Managers, you will gain hands-on experience across estimating, procurement, cost control, valuations and final accounts while receiving ongoing training and mentoring.

Key Responsibilities

  • Assist with preparing cost estimates and tender submissions.
  • Measure quantities from drawings and specifications.
  • Support preparation of Bills of Quantities (BOQs).
  • Obtain supplier and subcontractor quotations.
  • Assist with procurement of roofing materials.
  • Prepare interim valuations and payment applications.
  • Monitor project costs and budgets.
  • Assist with variations and final accounts.
  • Maintain commercial records and reports.
  • Attend site visits and project meetings.
  • Liaise with clients, suppliers and subcontractors.
  • Monitor labour, plant and material costs.
  • Ensure compliance with company procedures.
  • Assist with JCT and NEC contract administration.
  • Provide general commercial support.

Essential Skills & Experience

  • Degree, HNC/HND or equivalent in Quantity Surveying or Construction Management.
  • 1–3 years' Quantity Surveying experience.
  • Basic understanding of JCT and NEC contracts.
  • Knowledge of estimating, procurement and cost control.
  • Excellent Microsoft Excel skills.
  • Strong communication and organisational skills.
  • A proactive attitude with willingness to learn.
  • Full UK Driving Licence.

Desirable

  • Roofing or cladding experience.
  • Knowledge of BOQs and quantity take-offs.
  • Experience using Quantity Surveying software.
  • Knowledge of construction health & safety.
  • Working towards RICS or CIOB membership.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • 25 days annual leave plus Bank Holidays.
  • Training and mentoring.
  • Professional development support.
  • Career progression opportunities.
  • Friendly and supportive working environment.
  • Exposure to a variety of roofing projects.
