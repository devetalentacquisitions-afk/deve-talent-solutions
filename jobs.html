Quantity Surveyor(Roofing Construction Industry)

Industry: Construction

Location: London

Employment Type: Full-time

About the Role

We are looking for an experienced Quantity Surveyor to join our client's roofing division in London. You will be responsible for the commercial management of roofing projects from tender through to final account, ensuring projects are delivered on time, within budget, and to the highest commercial standards.

Key Responsibilities

  • Manage the commercial delivery of multiple roofing projects.
  • Prepare cost estimates, budgets, valuations, and final accounts.
  • Procure and manage subcontractors and suppliers.
  • Negotiate contracts, rates, and variations.
  • Monitor project costs, cash flow, and profitability.
  • Prepare CVRs, forecasts, and commercial reports.
  • Ensure compliance with JCT, NEC, and other contracts.
  • Identify and manage commercial risks.
  • Provide commercial support to project teams and clients.
  • Build and maintain strong client and supplier relationships.

Qualifications & Experience

  • Degree, HNC/HND, or equivalent in Quantity Surveying or a related discipline.
  • Minimum 5 years' Quantity Surveying experience (roofing preferred).
  • Strong knowledge of JCT and NEC contracts.
  • Excellent commercial, negotiation, and communication skills.
  • Proficient in Microsoft Office, particularly Excel.
  • Full UK driving licence.

Desirable

  • RICS membership or working towards chartership.
  • Experience across commercial, residential, industrial, or public sector projects.
  • Knowledge of roofing, cladding, waterproofing, or building envelope systems.

Salary & Benefits

  • £55,000 per annum.
  • Performance-related bonus.
  • Company pension.
  • 25 days' annual leave plus Bank Holidays.
  • Training and professional development.
  • Career progression opportunities.
