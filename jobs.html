About the Role

We are seeking a motivated and enthusiastic Junior Quantity Surveyor to join our client's growing commercial team division, based in London, within the roofing construction industry. This is an excellent opportunity for a recent graduate or an individual with 1–3 years' experience looking to develop their career in quantity surveying.

Working alongside experienced Quantity Surveyors and Project Managers, you will gain hands-on experience across all commercial aspects of roofing projects, from tendering and procurement through to cost control, valuations, and final accounts. You will receive ongoing training and support to develop your technical knowledge and commercial skills while contributing to the successful delivery of high-quality roofing projects.

Key Responsibilities

  • Assist with the preparation of cost estimates, budgets, and tender submissions for roofing projects.
  • Measure quantities from drawings, specifications, and site surveys.
  • Support the preparation of Bills of Quantities (BOQs), cost plans, and pricing schedules.
  • Obtain quotations from suppliers and subcontractors.
  • Assist in the procurement of roofing materials and subcontract packages.
  • Prepare documentation for interim valuations and payment applications.
  • Assess subcontractor applications and payment recommendations.
  • Monitor project costs, budgets, and cash flow.
  • Assist with variations, change orders, and final accounts.
  • Maintain accurate commercial records and reports.
  • Attend site visits and progress meetings.
  • Liaise with Project Managers, clients, suppliers, and subcontractors.
  • Monitor labour, material, and plant costs.
  • Ensure compliance with company procedures and health & safety requirements.
  • Assist with contract administration (JCT & NEC).
  • Carry out general commercial and administrative duties.

Skills & Experience

Essential

  • Degree, HNC/HND, or equivalent in Quantity Surveying, Construction Management, or similar.
  • 1–3 years' Quantity Surveying experience.
  • Basic understanding of JCT and NEC contracts.
  • Knowledge of estimating, procurement, cost control, and valuations.
  • Strong numerical and analytical skills.
  • Excellent Microsoft Excel skills.
  • Excellent communication skills.
  • Strong organisational and time management skills.
  • Proactive attitude and willingness to learn.
  • Full UK Driving Licence.

Desirable

  • Roofing, cladding or building envelope experience.
  • Knowledge of BOQs and quantity take-offs.
  • Experience with Quantity Surveying software.
  • Knowledge of construction health & safety.
  • Working towards RICS or CIOB membership.

What We Offer

  • Competitive salary of £30,000+ per annum.
  • Company benefits package.
  • Training, mentoring and professional development.
  • Excellent career progression opportunities.
  • Exposure to a variety of roofing projects.
  • Support towards professional qualifications.
  • Friendly and supportive working environment.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • 25 days annual leave plus Bank Holidays.
  • Work alongside experienced commercial teams.

  • Prepare CVRs, forecasts, and commercial reports.

  • Provide commercial support to project teams and clients.
  • Build and maintain strong client and supplier relationships.

  • Strong knowledge of JCT and NEC contracts.

  • Full UK driving licence.

Desirable

  • Knowledge of roofing, cladding, waterproofing, or building envelope systems.
