Who We Are

DEVE Talent Solutions is a specialist recruitment and executive search firm dedicated to delivering exceptional talent across the Mining, Oil & Gas, Infrastructure, Construction, Supply Chain, and Quantity Surveying sectors throughout the UK, Sub-Saharan Africa, and international markets.


We have successfully partnered with leading private organisations, multinational corporations, government ministries, and state-owned enterprises to build high-performing leadership teams and place experienced expatriate and local professionals in some of the most challenging and remote project locations across Africa.


Our expertise spans the entire project lifecycle — from exploration, engineering, procurement, construction, and production to operations, maintenance, and project delivery. We are committed to quality over quantity, conducting thorough market research, targeted headhunting, and comprehensive candidate assessments to identify professionals with the technical expertise, leadership capabilities, and cultural fit our clients require.


By specialising in niche and hard-to-fill positions, DEVE Talent Solutions consistently delivers recruitment solutions where conventional hiring methods often fall short. Our disciplined search methodology, extensive industry network, and commitment to excellence have made us a trusted recruitment partner for mining companies, oil and gas operators, EPC contractors, construction firms, infrastructure developers, and government ministries across Africa.


At DEVE Talent Solutions, we build lasting partnerships by providing world-class talent that drives operational excellence, sustainable growth, and long-term success for every client we serve.
