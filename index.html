About DEVE Talent Solutions

DEVE Talent Solutions is a specialist recruitment and executive search firm delivering world-class professionals to organizations operating across the UK and international markets.

We have successfully partnered with leading private organizations, multinational corporations, government ministries, and state-owned enterprises to build high-performing leadership teams and recruit experienced expatriate and local professionals for some of the United Kingdom's and Africa's most challenging and remote project locations.

Our expertise spans the complete project lifecycle—from exploration, engineering, procurement, construction, and production to operations, maintenance, and project delivery.

By combining market intelligence, targeted headhunting, and rigorous candidate assessment, we identify professionals with the technical expertise, leadership capability, and cultural fit our clients require.
